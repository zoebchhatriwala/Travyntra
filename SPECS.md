# Travyntra — Corporate Travel Infrastructure

## 1. Project Vision
Travyntra is a multi-tenant travel management ecosystem. It bridges the gap between Travel Agencies (Agencies), Corporations (Companies), and their Staff (Employees). The system facilitates seamless journey planning, hierarchical approval workflows, and centralized financial operations.

## 2. Technical Architecture
- **Framework**: Next.js 15 (App Router)
- **Styling**: Vanilla CSS Modules (Design System approach)
- **Database**: PostgreSQL via Prisma ORM
- **Authentication**: NextAuth.js (Role-Based & Company-Aware)
- **Multi-tenancy**: 
    - Support for Company-specific portals via path-based routing (`/company/[slug]`) and subdomain support (`[slug].travyntra.com`).
    - Company-specific registration pages.

## 3. Design Aesthetics & UI System
- **Theme**: Strictly Light Mode (High clarity, professional yet accessible).
- **Core Palette**:
    - **Backgrounds**: Soft, airy whites and joyful pastels (Mint, Sky Blue, Soft Lavender).
    - **Typography**: Deep Charcoal/Black text for maximum readability.
    - **Accents**: Vibrant but friendly primary colors (Vivid Cobalt, Warm Amber).
- **Feel**: "Corporate Joy." The interface should feel premium and reliable but move away from cold corporate design towards a warm, collaborative environment.
- **UI Elements**: Large border radii (soft corners), subtle glassmorphism, and generous white space.

## 4. Entity Categories & Multi-Tenancy
- **Company**: The source of travel requests. They represent organizations where employees work.
- **Agency**: Fulfillment entities that bid on and fulfill travel requests. They have subscriptions.
- **Request Manager**: A role (either Super Admin or Company Admin, depending on setup) that manages bids from different Agencies for a single request.

## 5. User Roles & Workspace Hierarchy

### A. The Super Admin (Platform Owner)
- **Workspace**: Platform Administration (`/admin/dashboard`)
- **Capabilities**:
    - **Vetting**: Approve or Reject new Agency registrations.
    - **Company Activation**: Verify and activate corporate accounts (Company Admins).
    - **Global Oversight**: High-level view of platform activity and health.

### B. The Agency (Fulfillment Entity)
*Entities like TravelMart who provide fulfillment services.*
- **Workspace**: Agency Console (`/agent/dashboard`)
- **Capabilities**:
    - **Bidding**: Bid on requests from Companies.
    - **Fulfillment Console**: Accept approved requests if bid is accepted, upload Tickets/Visas.
    - **Subscription**: Managed by Super Admin (Free, Starter, Enterprise plans).
    - **Finance Module**: Generate Invoices, track pending receivables across assigned companies.
    - **Support**: Participate in communication threads for active requests.

### C. The Company Admin (Company Owner)
*Organizations like Amazon or local enterprises.*
- **Access**: `/company/[slug]/admin`
- **Capabilities**:
    - **Domain Setup**: Configure company domains for auto-association.
    - **Staff Management**: Approve/Reject employee registrations.
    - **Role Assignment**: Assign "Manager" or "Approver" roles to specific staff.
    - **Workflow Designer**: Construct a custom chain of command (e.g., Employee -> Manager -> HR -> Agency).
    - **Billing**: View invoices generated by the Agency and track internal spending.

### D. The Employee (Staff)
*Individuals requesting travel.*
- **Access**: `/company/[slug]/signup` and `/company/[slug]/dashboard`
- **Capabilities**:
    - **Self-Registration**: Sign up under their specific company portal.
    - **Trip Requests**: Initiate new requests with dynamic preferences.
    - **Asset Vault**: Download tickets/visas once fulfilled.
    - **Collaboration**: Direct communication with managers and agencies via the request thread.

### E. The Request Manager
*A role that mediates between Company requests and Agency fulfillments.*
- **Capabilities**:
    - **Bid Management**: View multiple bids for a request.
    - **Fulfillment Assignment**: Accept one bid (which assigns the Agency) and automatically rejects others.

## 6. Feature Specifications

### 🛡 The Hierarchical Workflow Engine
- Requests do not go to the Agency immediately.
- They must pass through the company's internal "Chain of Approval."
- **Example Flow**: `Request` -> `Reporting Manager` -> `Department Head` -> `Company Admin` -> `Fulfillment Agency`.
- If any stage rejects, the flow breaks and notifies the employee.

### 💬 Request-Specific Communication Threads
- Each request generates a dedicated workspace.
- **Activity Log**: Every status change, comment, and file upload is tracked in an immutable audit trail.
- **Contextual Chat**: Real-time communication between the Employee, their internal Approvers, and the fulfillment Agency.
- **File Management**: Secure upload/download of Passports, Visas, and Tickets.

### ✅ Fulfillment & Closure
- A request is only "Complete" when the Agency uploads the final artifacts (Ticket/Visa).
- Marking a request as "Done" triggers the invoice generation prompt for the Agency.
- Final documents are archived in the Employee's travel history.

### 💰 Financials & Invoicing
- **Agency View**: Track pending invoices, generate new ones per request, and view global revenue.
- **Company View**: Centralized "Invoices" section to track liabilities and historical spend.
- **Direct Link**: Every invoice is tied back to a specific Trip Request for reconciliation.
